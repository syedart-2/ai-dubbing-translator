## 2026-10-18 · b27: English idiom translated word for word

**What went wrong.** "at your own pace" was written `اپنی رفتار سے`, a word-for-word copy. رفتار is physical speed, which is not what the phrase means here.

**How it was caught.** The reviewer rejected the line.

**The check that prevents it.** "at your own pace" is `اپنی سہولت سے`. More generally, never translate an English idiom word for word: translate what it does in the sentence. Check every idiom against this file before delivering.

## 2026-10-18 · b111: English filler "you know" kept as آپ جانتے ہیں

**What went wrong.** The filler "you know" was translated literally. It is an English speech habit and carries no meaning.

**How it was caught.** Reviewer comment.

**The check that prevents it.** Drop "you know", "I mean" and "well" where they are only fillers, or replace with a natural Urdu filler such as دیکھیں. Never آپ جانتے ہیں.
